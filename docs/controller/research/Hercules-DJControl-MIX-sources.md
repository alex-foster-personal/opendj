## WebMIDI device name

The USB port enumerates as "DJControl MIX" or "Hercules DJControl MIX MIDI 1".
`nameMatch: 'DJControl MIX(?:$| MIDI)'` (regex_lite-safe) rejects "DJControl MIX Ultra", "DJControl Inpulse 200" and "DJConsole RMX2". No unit was plugged in during this trace.

## Hardware capture before bindings

Before any hint becomes a binding, capture live MIDI from a real DJControl MIX with `tools/controller-probe/capture.py` and verify every wire in the map against the trace. Promote to `DEVICE_MAP_REGISTRY` only after tier-3 confirmation.
