## WebMIDI device name

The USB port enumerates as "MixTrack Pro FX". `nameMatch: 'Mixtrack\s*Pro\s*FX'` (case-insensitive) misses the 2011 "Mixtrack Pro", the "Mixtrack Pro II" and "Mixtrack Pro 3", and the "Mixtrack Platinum FX". No unit was plugged in: nothing here is hardware-confirmed.

## Runtime map

`apps/webui/frontend/src/lib/rb/midi/maps/numark-mixtrack-pro-fx.ts` exports `NUMARK_MIXTRACK_PRO_FX_MAP`: **hints only, no bindings** (ADR-0137 house rule: no fake behaviour from unverified wires). Channel layout per the Mixxx mapping: deck transport, jog, pitch and mixer strip on channels 1 / 2; pads, pad-mode and loop buttons on channels 5 / 6; effect units on channels 9 / 10; MASTER, CUE GAIN, CUE MIX on channel 15; BROWSE, LOAD and the crossfader on channel 16. LED rules follow the script's writes (same notes as the inputs, 0x7F on, 0x01 dim) and the VU meters are CC 31 on channels 1 / 2.

The map is registered for the practice route only (`PRACTICE_DEVICE_MAPS`), not in `DEVICE_MAP_REGISTRY`: the engine export requires at least one binding per registered map, and a real unit should keep landing in the learn log as unmapped until a capture (tier 3, `tools/controller-probe/capture.py`) promotes hints to bindings.
