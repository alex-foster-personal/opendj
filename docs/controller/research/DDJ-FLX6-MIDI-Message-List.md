## WebMIDI device name

Runtime map selection uses `nameMatch: 'DDJ-FLX6'` against the WebMIDI port name, which also matches the DDJ-FLX6-GT variant (same panel and MIDI list).

## Runtime map

`apps/webui/frontend/src/lib/rb/midi/maps/ddj-flx6.ts` exports `FLX6_MAP`: four decks on channels 1-4 (two physical strips, the DECK button swaps channel), 4-channel mixer with FILTER on channel 7 CC 23-26, hot-cue and beat-loop pads on channels 8/10/12/14, jog platter and wheel side (CC 33/34/35, relative centred), browse + LOAD 1-4, crossfader, master, headphone mix and level, master cue, BEAT SYNC, channel level meters (MIDI-OUT CC 2). Everything else on the sheet is a hint. No hardware capture yet: every binding is INFERRED from the PDF.
