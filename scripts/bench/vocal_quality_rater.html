Stem Quality Rater

Rate each clip 1-10 on how clean the isolated stem sounds. Default order is the machine ranking: best first, worst second, then the rest in descending order, so the extremes are easy to A/B.

The waveform is the player. Click anywhere on a strip to play from that point, click it again to pause. Cmd+click (Ctrl+click on Windows or Linux) any arm stem to hear the TRUE stem of the same colour at the same instant, or hold Cmd/Ctrl while an arm plays to swap to the truth live and back on release. The strip that is sounding carries a ring and a badge: white for an arm, orange for the truth.

How this works

  1. Clips are laid out in machine-score order: best, then worst, then ranks 2 downward. Scores, SI-SDR and demucs settings are on show.
  2. Rate every clip 1-10 for the stem under test, and 1-10 for the instrumental wherever an instrumental player is shown.
  3. Want an unbiased test instead? Hit Blind mode at the top: scores and settings hide, the order shuffles, and clips become anonymous letters.
  4. The summary table pairs your score with the machine score and the delta. In blind mode it stays hidden until every clip is rated.

How to rate: play the reference at the top, then each clip, and judge how close that clip gets to the reference. The reference is what a 10 sounds like.

What to listen for

Bad, mark down for these

  • metallic / robotic / phasey ringing (aliasing) on sustained notes
  • warbling or underwater smearing, especially on held vowels
  • pre-echo: a faint ghost of a word just before it lands
  • smeared or lisping consonants; lost sibilance ("s" turning to mush)
  • dropouts: quiet vocal phrases deleted entirely, or gating/pumping between words
  • bleed: kick, hats, bass or synth stabs audible in the vocal stem
  • hollow/comb-filtered tone, like the vocal is in a pipe
  • abruptly truncated reverb or delay tails (cut off rather than decaying)

Good, do not mark down for these

  • the vocal's own reverb and delay tails decaying naturally, a good stem keeps them
  • breaths, lip noise, room tone around the voice
  • natural sibilance and consonant bite
  • background/harmony vocals and adlibs kept with the lead

Rating the instrumental

Judge it for vocal bleed left behind (words or syllables still audible in the music) and for holes where the vocal was carved out of the backing.

Reverb and echo that belong to the voice are part of the vocal: a stem that strips them is worse, not cleaner.
